Reduce Paper, Go Electronic with Signulu

June 21, 2021  •  4 min read

Despite the rise of digital technology, most businesses still generate enormous amounts of paper. Forms, contracts, invoices, approvals, and receipts pile up in filing cabinets and storage rooms, consuming physical space, staff time, and organizational budget. The transition to a paperless office isn't just environmentally responsible — it's a strategic business advantage.

Signulu makes going paperless simple, practical, and secure. Here's why the time to make the switch is now.

The True Cost of Paper

Most businesses underestimate their true paper costs. Beyond the obvious printing expenses, paper-based processes carry hidden costs:

  • Storage: Physical filing systems occupy valuable office space that could be used more productively
  • Staff time: Employees spend hours printing, filing, retrieving, and re-filing documents
  • Error correction: Paper documents get misfiled, lost, or damaged — requiring costly recreations
  • Courier costs: Shipping physical documents for signatures is expensive and slow
  • Compliance risk: Paper records are harder to audit and easier to tamper with
Research Insight: The average office worker uses 10,000 sheets of paper per year. Going paperless with Signulu can cut document-related costs by 40–60%.

How Signulu Powers the Paperless Office

  • e-Signatures: Replace wet-ink signatures with legally valid digital signatures on any document type — contracts, agreements, approvals, and more.
  • Document Templates: Create standard templates for frequently used documents. No more reprinting forms with minor changes.
  • Cloud Storage: All signed documents are stored securely in the cloud — no physical filing needed. Search, retrieve, and share instantly.
  • WhatsApp Integration: Send documents for signature via WhatsApp — meeting signers where they already are, no printer needed.
  • Workflow Automation: Multi-step approval processes happen digitally, with automatic routing and reminders — no paper routing slips needed.

Environmental Impact

Beyond business efficiency, reducing paper consumption has measurable environmental benefits. Every ream of paper saved means fewer trees cut, less water used in manufacturing, and less CO₂ emitted in production and transportation. Organizations that commit to going paperless with Signulu can quantify and report their environmental savings — a growing requirement for ESG reporting.

Getting Started with a Paperless Transition

The key to a successful paperless transition is starting with the highest-volume document processes. For most organizations, these are:

  • Employee onboarding documents
  • Vendor contracts and purchase orders
  • Customer agreements and invoices
  • Internal approval forms

With Signulu, you can digitize these processes within days — not months. The platform requires no installation, integrates with existing workflows, and provides training resources to bring your team up to speed quickly.

Start with the Lite plan (free, 5 documents/month) to see how Signulu works in your organization, then scale to the plan that fits your volume.
