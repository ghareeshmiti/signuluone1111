Construction Plan Approval from Multiple Stakeholders in a Jiffy

August 25, 2021  •  5 min read

The construction industry is well-known for its complex approval processes. From architectural blueprints to safety compliance documents, every plan requires sign-off from multiple stakeholders — architects, engineers, project managers, site supervisors, and clients. Traditional paper-based approvals slow down projects, introduce errors, and make it nearly impossible to track who approved what and when.

Signulu transforms this cumbersome process into a streamlined, digital workflow that gets approvals done in a fraction of the time.

The Challenge: Multi-Stakeholder Approvals in Construction

Construction projects involve dozens of documents that need approvals at different stages:

  • Architectural and structural drawings
  • Material procurement orders
  • Subcontractor agreements
  • Safety compliance certificates
  • Change orders and variation requests
  • Handover and completion certificates

When these approvals depend on physical presence or couriered documents, a single stakeholder's unavailability can delay an entire project by days or weeks.

Key Insight: A typical construction project loses 15–20% of its schedule to document approval delays. Digitizing this process with Signulu can recover that time.

How Signulu Solves Construction Approvals

Signulu's e-signature platform is designed for exactly this kind of multi-party, sequential approval workflow:

  • Sequential Signing Workflows: Set up a signing order so each stakeholder receives the document only after the previous one has signed. Architects sign first, then structural engineers, then the project manager.
  • Parallel Signing: When multiple parties can review independently, send the document to all of them simultaneously and collect approvals in parallel.
  • Automated Reminders: Signulu automatically sends reminders to pending signers, eliminating the need for manual follow-ups.
  • Real-Time Status Tracking: See at a glance which stakeholder has signed, who is pending, and what the estimated completion time is.
  • Audit Trail: Every action — viewing, signing, commenting — is timestamped and logged, creating a tamper-proof record for compliance and dispute resolution.

Interior Design and Real Estate Approvals

The same workflow applies to interior design projects and real estate developments, where clients need to approve floor plans, material selections, and design iterations. With Signulu, an interior designer can send multiple design options to a client, collect feedback annotations, and get a final approved signature — all without a single in-person meeting.

Compliance and Legal Validity

Signulu's e-signatures are legally valid under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India) and equivalent laws in other jurisdictions. Every signed document is sealed with a digital certificate, making it court-admissible evidence in case of disputes.

Getting Started

Construction firms can get started with Signulu in minutes. Upload your plan documents in PDF format, define the signing workflow, and send it out. Your stakeholders sign from any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile — without needing to install any software.

The result: faster approvals, reduced project delays, and a complete digital audit trail that protects everyone involved.
