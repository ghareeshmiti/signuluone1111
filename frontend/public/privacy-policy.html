This Privacy Policy explains how Signulu ("we", "us") handles personal data when you visit our website, create an account, or sign a document sent through our platform.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Signulu website and the Signulu electronic signature service. Where a customer sends you a document through Signulu, that customer determines the purpose of the document and acts as the controller of its content; we process it on their behalf.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — name, work email, company, role and billing details.
  • Document data — files you upload, field values, and the recipients you specify.
  • Signature evidence — timestamps, IP address, device and browser information, and consent records captured at signing.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, features used and diagnostic logs.
  • Communications — messages you send to sales or support.

3. How we use data

We use personal data to provide and secure the service, to authenticate signers, to produce the audit certificate attached to each completed document, to bill customers, to provide support, and to improve reliability and performance. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use document content to train models.

4. Sharing and processors

We share data with vetted service providers who host our infrastructure, deliver email and process payments, strictly under contract and only as needed to run the service. We may disclose data where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

5. Retention

Documents and their audit trails are retained for as long as the customer's account requires, or according to the retention rules that customer configures. Account and billing records are kept as long as required by applicable law. On verified request, we delete or return data as described in our agreements.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Access is role-based and logged, completed documents are sealed with a tamper-evident hash, and we review our controls regularly. No system is perfectly secure, so we also maintain incident response procedures.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. If your data was submitted through a customer's document, we will refer your request to that customer.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@signulu.com or through our contact page.
