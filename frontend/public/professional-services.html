Why it matters

Talent will not wait for paperwork

Identifying, screening and interviewing candidates has become harder, and when you cannot meet people in person it is harder still to have them sign the forms a visit to your office would normally cover.

Recruiters deal with dozens of candidates who are asked to review and sign non-disclosure agreements, background checks and immigration forms — usually as soon as possible. eSignature makes those tasks secure, timely and efficient.
